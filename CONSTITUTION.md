# Medicina / 10X Living constitution (fence, not a parent)

Founder-written starting text. Changes need 95% of members.

## One law
Enjoy life. Do not steal someone else's enjoyment.

## The fence (this is what we police)
1. Consent. No means no. Children are protected first.
2. No violence, threats, or coercion.
3. Do not poison water, soil, wildlife, or start reckless fire.
4. Do not trap people with debt, rumor-as-punishment, or forced attendance.
5. Harm is repaired. We do not shame or exile as sport.

## Not on this fence
How you dress, love, pray, celebrate, work, or rest — unless it crosses the fence.
There is no lifestyle police and no founder-king for taste.

## Power
The assembly of residents is the boss of shared land and money.
An administrator pays bills and keeps checklists. They cannot invent new morals.
