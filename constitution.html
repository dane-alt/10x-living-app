Constitution

Full text also lives in CONSTITUTION.md in this project.

One law

Enjoy life. Do not steal someone else’s enjoyment.

The fence

  1. Consent. Children first.
  2. No violence, threats, or coercion.
  3. Do not poison water, soil, wildlife, or start reckless fire.
  4. No debt-traps, rumor-as-punishment, or forced attendance.
  5. Harm is repaired. No shame sport.

Not the fence

Dress, love, pray, celebrate, work, rest — unless they cross the fence.

Only metric: well-being per minute of attention. Close the tab when life is happening.
